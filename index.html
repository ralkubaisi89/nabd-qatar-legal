Nabd Qatar

Qatar, Gulf and world news, as it happens.

About

Nabd Qatar brings together news from more than 50 trusted publishers — local, Gulf and international — in one place, in Arabic and English. For every story the app shows the headline and a short excerpt with the original publisher's name and publication date, and a direct link to read the full story on the publisher's site. Full rights to every story belong to its publisher.

Developer and publisher: Rashid Alkubaisi — Qatar.
